Tsuiwan

Ingredients

  • meat(could be beef, sheep, goat, horse)
  • vegetables(potato, carrot, onion)
  • flour
  • vegetable or meat oil

Steps

  1. chop meat and potato middle sized cube
  2. chop carrot and onion thin narrow
  3. make dough with water and let it rest a little
  4. divide dough to little parts that you can roll out
  5. oil those flattened dough and put them together up vertically
  6. slice them to little pieces like fried rice but thinner
  7. heat up oil and put onion
  8. after it got brownish put meat in
  9. after the meat got brownish put vegetables
  10. fry little bit and put water in
  11. put slices dough on top of it but leave a circle without dough so steam can go up and cook it from the top
  12. wait until dough is cooked
  13. take out some soup it there is and mix everything together
