Ingredients

  • meat(could be beef, sheep, goat)
  • onion
  • flour

Steps

  1. make flattened liitle circle doughs with water
  2. put minced meat and onion together and some salt and pepper(if you want sth special u can put whatever u want just search it up)
  3. put meat in dough and close it beautifully
  4. steam it for 20 minutes
  5. fan little when openning the lid after steaming
  6. enjoy
